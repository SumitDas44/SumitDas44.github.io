## Overview

Designed a 220V AC to 5–35V regulated DC supply using LM317T regulators, LM358 op-amps, relays, and SCRs.

## Key details

Implemented short-circuit and over/under-voltage protection; simulated in Proteus and built on Veroboard.

**Technologies:** Proteus, LM317T, LM358, Electronics.
