## Overview

Designed and simulated a 2.5–4 GHz ultra-wideband Vivaldi antenna in CST Studio Suite.

## Key details

Achieved 3.2 GHz resonance, −47.06 dB return loss, approximately 1 VSWR, and 10.25 dBi directivity at 4 GHz.

**Technologies:** CST Studio Suite, Antenna Design, UWB.
